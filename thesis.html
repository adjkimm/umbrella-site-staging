Thesis

We own and operate good businesses with our own permanent capital, holding each as long as it earns its keep.

Principles

We are owner-operators.

We buy businesses to run them, not to trade them. The people who own the business answer for how it is run.

We do not flip companies.

We invest our own money.

There is no fund, no outside capital, no carry, and no deployment deadline. Nobody can force a sale to meet a schedule.

We do not answer to outside investors.

A business that earns its keep is a complete success.

We do not need every company to become enormous. A business that earns its keep year after year is a complete success, not a stepping stone.

We do not chase growth for its own sake.

We intend to hold indefinitely.

Permanent capital lets us decide in decades instead of quarters: invest through cycles, back good operators, and let results compound quietly.

We do not work against a clock.

These are statements of intent, not promises. Circumstances change, and we will say so plainly when they do.
